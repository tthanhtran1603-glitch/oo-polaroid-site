Not Instax.
Not a photobooth.

Most "instant" photos in Vietnam are digital prints or standard Instax film. O-O Polaroid uses genuine analog Polaroid cameras — every shot is a real chemical reaction, one of a kind, no copies.

The camera auto-focuses and auto-exposes, so you shoot the moment yourself. Want company? Add the Premium package and a photographer will guide the shot for you.
